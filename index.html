公司簡介About the Company

海天集團國際有限公司Holden Group (International) Limited

海天集團於2024年6月在香港註冊成立，是一家專注於高端食材供應鏈的專業公司。公司立足香港，以P2P（點對點）模式為核心，直接連接優質源頭與終端客戶，主要服務中餐廳、日本餐廳、西餐廳、五星級酒店及高端個人客戶。

Holden Group was incorporated in Hong Kong in June 2024 as a specialist in premium food supply. Built around a P2P (point-to-point) model, we connect premium sources directly with end clients, mainly Chinese, Japanese and Western restaurants, five-star hotels and private clients.

我們致力透過專業化營運、嚴格品質管控與高效物流配送，協助客戶提升出品穩定性與營運效率。

Through professional operations, strict quality control and efficient logistics, we help clients serve more consistently and run more efficiently.
